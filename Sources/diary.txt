16/02/26
========

1. Looking at using the two line sensors as analogue sensors rather than clamping then to digital values.
2. A/D unit : 3.3v input, 8-bit conversion (capable of 10-bit)
            : A/D value of BLACK tape :3.24v = (3.24/3.3)*2.55 = 250 (Digital `1`)
            : A/D value of WHITE paper : 0.96v = (0.96/3.3)*255 = 74 (Digital `0`)
            : Midpoint = ((250 - 74)/2) + 74 = 162
            : Worst case error is (250 - 74) = 176
